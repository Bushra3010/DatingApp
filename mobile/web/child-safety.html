Child Safety Standards

Last updated: 26 November 2026

These standards apply to Everlane: Dating (the Everlane app on Google Play and on the web) and everyone who uses it.

Zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation

Everlane strictly prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). On Everlane you must never:

  • Create, upload, share, request or link to any sexual or sexualised image, video, audio or text involving a person under 18.
  • Groom, sexually solicit, or try to contact or meet a person under 18.
  • Sexualise minors in profiles, photos, prompts, messages or Hangouts.
  • Traffic, sextort or otherwise exploit a child, or help anyone else do so.

Breaking these rules leads to an immediate, permanent ban, removal of the content, and a report to the authorities.

Adults only

Everlane is only for people aged 18 and over. Every member gives their date of birth at sign-up, and accounts under 18 cannot be created. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we remove it.

How we prevent and detect abuse

  • Profile photos can be checked against a live selfie before a profile gets the verified badge.
  • Messages and profile text are filtered for banned words.
  • Every profile and chat has a Report option, and reports are reviewed by our team.
  • Staff can suspend or block accounts, remove photos and preserve evidence.

How to report

  • In the app: open the person’s profile or chat, tap Report, and choose the reason that fits. Then block them.
  • By email: write to our child safety contact at nandini99yadav@gmail.com. Please include the profile name and what happened. Do not send or forward abusive images.
  • If a child is in immediate danger, call 112. You can also call Childline on 1098, or report online at cybercrime.gov.in.

What we do with reports

We review child safety reports as a priority. When we find CSAE or CSAM, we remove it, permanently ban the accounts involved, preserve the evidence, and report it to the relevant authorities, including the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal in India and the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC). We comply with the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act, 2012, the Information Technology Act, 2000 and other applicable child safety laws.

Child safety contact

Our designated contact for child safety and CSAM prevention can be reached at nandini99yadav@gmail.com.
