Community Guidelines

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Be kind

Treat everyone with respect. No harassment, hate, threats or unwanted sexual messages.

Be real

Use your own photos and your real age. No fake profiles, impersonation or catfishing.

Stay safe

Never send money or share OTPs, UPI or bank details. Meet in public places and tell a friend where you’ll be.

No selling

No promotions, escort services, scams or links to other platforms.

Adults only

Everlane is for people 18 and over. We remove anyone younger.

Reporting

Report anything that breaks these rules from the person’s profile. Repeated reports lead to suspension.
