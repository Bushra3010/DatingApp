Who we are

Everlane is a dating app for adults in India, available on the web at everlane-topaz.vercel.app and as a mobile app. It is operated by [company legal name], [registered address] ("Everlane", "we", "us").

This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it and the rights you have under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act). By creating an account you give consent to the processing described here. You can withdraw that consent at any time by deleting your account.

Questions: [support email].

Information you give us

  • Account: your email address, your phone number (if you sign up with a one-time code by SMS), or your Google account's email and name (if you sign in with Google). If you use email, your password is handled by our authentication provider and we never see it in plain text.
  • Profile: name, date of birth (to show your age and confirm you are 18 or over), gender, who you want to meet, height, languages, city, job, bio, prompt answers, interests, photos and, if you choose, a recorded voice prompt.
  • Compatibility quiz: your answers, used to suggest people and show how well you match.
  • Activity: the people you like and pass on, your matches and the messages you send.
  • Hangouts: plans you host (type, title, time, area, exact venue, city, notes, number of spots) and requests you send or accept to join plans.
  • Verification: if you ask for the verified badge, a short burst of live selfie frames from your front camera.
  • Safety: reports you make, and anything you tell us when you contact support.

Location

We only use your location at the level of a city.

  • Your city: you pick it from a list. If you tap "use my current location", your device works out the nearest listed city and only that city is saved. We do not store your exact coordinates for this.
  • Distance: distances to other people are calculated from city centres, not from where you actually are.
  • Crossed paths (optional): if you turn this on, the app saves the roughly 1 km area you are in, with the date, so it can show people who were in the same area on the same day. It is off until you share your area, older areas are deleted after 30 days, and you can clear all of them at any time.
  • Hangouts: other members see a plan's general area. The exact venue is shown only to the host and to guests the host has accepted.

Sample profiles

Alongside real members, Everlane runs some sample profiles that we create ourselves. They are labelled "Sample profile" in the app. They are not real people: their replies are automatic, and they may like you, match with you or accept a Hangouts request automatically. Messages you send to a sample profile are stored like any other message so the conversation works, and are handled under this policy. Please do not share personal details with them or arrange to meet them.

How we use your information

  • To create and run your account and sign you in.
  • To show your profile to other members and suggest people to you, based on your preferences, city, distance and quiz answers.
  • To deliver likes, matches, messages and Hangouts.
  • To verify that profiles belong to real people.
  • To keep Everlane safe: filtering banned words from messages and profile text, reviewing reports, and acting against accounts that break our Terms.
  • To manage premium plans.
  • To answer your questions and tell you about important changes.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

What other members see

Members you are shown to can see your profile: name, age (not your full date of birth), gender, height, languages, city, distance, job, bio, prompts, interests, photos, voice prompt and verified badge. Your messages are seen by the person you send them to. Your email address and phone number are never shown to other members.

Safety and moderation

  • Messages and profile text containing words on our banned list are blocked.
  • When you report someone, we keep the report, its reason and details, and a copy of the last few messages between you, so our team can review what happened.
  • Our admins can see reports and the attached messages, and can warn, suspend or block accounts. They do not read conversations that have not been reported.
  • Verification selfies are stored in a private storage area that only our admins can open. An admin compares them with your profile photos to decide whether to give you the verified badge.
  • We may keep or share information where the law requires it, or where it is needed to protect someone's safety.

Who we share data with

We use a small number of service providers (data processors) who handle data only on our instructions:

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and file storage for profiles, photos, voice prompts, messages and verification selfies.
  • Vercel: hosting for the Everlane web app.
  • Google: only if you choose "Sign in with Google", to confirm your identity.
  • An SMS provider used by our sign-in service, only if you sign up with your phone number, to send your one-time code.

These providers may store or process data on servers outside India. We share data with authorities only when required by law. If Everlane is ever sold or merged, your data would move to the new owner under this policy, and we would tell you first.

How long we keep data

We keep your data while your account is open.

When you tap Delete account in Settings, your account is deleted along with your profile, likes, passes, matches, messages, quiz answers, Hangouts and crossed-paths areas. Your photos and voice prompt are removed from storage. Messages you sent are removed from the other person's chat too.

We may keep a limited record for longer where we need it for safety or legal reasons, for example reports about an account that was suspended or blocked, or where the law requires us to keep it. Verification selfies are deleted once they are no longer needed for verification. Backups held by our providers are overwritten on their normal schedule.

Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you have the right to:

  • Access: ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we use it.
  • Correction and updating: most of your profile can be edited in the app at any time; you can ask us to correct anything else.
  • Erasure: delete your account in Settings, or ask us to delete specific data.
  • Withdraw consent: you can stop at any time by deleting your account. This does not affect processing done before.
  • Grievance redressal: complain to our Grievance Officer (below), and, if you are not satisfied, to the Data Protection Board of India.
  • Nominate: name someone who can exercise these rights for you if you die or are unable to.

To use any of these rights, write to [support email] from the email or phone number linked to your account. We may need to confirm it is you before we act.
