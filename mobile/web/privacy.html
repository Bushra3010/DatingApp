Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to operate our matchmaking, compatibility, and social meetup services:

  • Account Information: We collect your name, age, date of birth, gender, phone number, email address, photographs, bio, and dating preferences during registration.
  • Verification Data: If you apply for a verified profile badge, we process your verification selfie or identity confirmation media strictly to authenticate your profile.
  • Compatibility Data: We process your questionnaire responses and interaction preferences to generate area-by-area Chemistry reports.
  • Location Data: With your permission, we collect precise or approximate location data to show nearby profiles and facilitate local Hangouts.
  • Payment Information: When you purchase a paid plan via a UPI app, we collect transaction reference numbers (UTR), payment screenshots, or UPI IDs required for manual verification.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process your personal data strictly for the following purposes:

  • Core Matchmaking: Operating your profile, delivering daily likes, sending weekly Sparks, applying monthly profile boosts, and calculating Chemistry reports.
  • Hangouts Feature: Enabling eligible members to host or join plans nearby and coordinate local meetups with other users.
  • Payment Verification: Manually verifying your UPI payment, which typically takes a few hours before your upgraded plan turns on.
  • Platform Safety: Detecting bots, preventing fraud, enforcing community guidelines, and investigating reported accounts.

Automated profiles

Alongside real members, Everlane runs some automated profiles that we create ourselves. They are labelled "Automated profile" in the app and are not real people; their replies are automatic. Messages you send to an automated profile are stored like any other message so the conversation works, and are handled under this policy. Please do not share personal details with them.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data to third-party advertisers or data brokers. We share data only in the following contexts:

  • With Other Users: Your public profile, photos, verification badge, and hosted Hangouts activity are visible to other platform members.
  • Service Providers: We use trusted hosting, database, and communication providers bound by strict confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal Compliance: We may disclose information if required by Indian law, court order, or law enforcement agencies investigating cybercrime or safety threats.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your personal data only as long as your account remains active. When you delete your account, your profile, photos, and chat history are permanently erased from our active databases within 30 days, except for transaction records required for tax or legal compliance (retained for up to 7 years).

5. Your Rights Under the DPDP Act

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you hold the following rights regarding your data:

  • Right to Access: You may request a summary of the personal data we process and any third parties with whom it is shared.
  • Right to Correction: You may update inaccurate or incomplete personal details directly through your profile settings.
  • Right to Erasure: You may delete your account or request complete erasure of your personal data at any time.
  • Consent Withdrawal: You may revoke permissions such as location access via your device settings, though location-based matching will be limited.

6. Security Safeguards

We implement encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS), database access controls, and secure authentication protocols to protect your personal information against unauthorized access or disclosure.
