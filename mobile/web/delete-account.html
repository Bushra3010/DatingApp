Delete it in the app

  1. Open Everlane (the Android app or everlanedating.com) and sign in.
  2. Open the menu and go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Can’t sign in?

What is deleted

  • Your profile, photos, voice prompt, quiz answers, likes, matches, messages, Hangouts and location data.

  • Payment and transaction records, for up to 7 years, where tax or other laws require it.
